Privacy Policy

Apex Elite Mechanical LLC · Last updated July 2026

What we collect

When you book a visit or contact us, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about your HVAC equipment. When you book through our online calendar, that booking is processed by Cal.com under their own privacy terms.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm, and perform your service visit
  • To send your digital system condition report
  • To maintain your equipment's service history if you're a member
  • To respond when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties except the service providers needed to operate (scheduling, email, payments).

Analytics

We use privacy-conscious, cookieless analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) to understand how the site is used — page views and button clicks. It does not track you across other websites and does not use advertising cookies.

Photos

Service photos we take document your equipment's condition and are delivered to you in your report. We will not publish photos from your home in marketing without asking you first.
