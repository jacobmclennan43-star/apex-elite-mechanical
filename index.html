Good to know — what these prices do and don't include

  • No travel fees anywhere in our Maryland / DMV service area.
  • These are cleaning & inspection services. Repairs, replacement parts, and refrigerant work are not included — if we find an issue, it's documented in your report with a recommendation.
  • "Accessible evaporator cleaning" means cleaning the coil surfaces reachable without opening refrigerant lines or removing the coil.
  • Blower-wheel removal is included in Premium Deep Clean only.
  • Severely neglected equipment can take additional time — any extra work is always quoted and approved before it begins.
  • Every price is confirmed up front. No estimates, no meter, no surprise line items.

Why Maintenance Matters

Small maintenance issues become expensive system problems.

Restricted airflow, dirty coils, drainage problems, and worn electrical components can reduce comfort and place unnecessary strain on HVAC equipment. Apex documents these conditions before they become surprises.

52%

Space heating and air conditioning together account for 52% of a U.S. household's annual energy consumption (2020) — how well your system runs is real money.

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration

5–15%

Routinely replacing or cleaning a dirty air filter can lower an air conditioner's energy consumption by 5–15 percent.

Source: U.S. Dept. of Energy — Home Cooling 101

20–30%

In homes with ducts, about 20 to 30 percent of the air that moves through the duct system is lost to leaks, holes, and poorly connected ducts.

Source: ENERGY STAR
